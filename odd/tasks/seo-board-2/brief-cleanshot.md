<b>H2: Copy text from a screenshot</b>

- Vision en el Mac, en orden de lectura, aviso «Copied N characters». La imagen no sale del Mac. ⚠️ Idiomas y QR: no documentado; no comparar con «30+ idiomas».

<b>H2: Pin a screenshot</b>

- Ventana sin bordes siempre encima; zoom 25–400 %; Esc cierra; varias a la vez. CleanShot además tiene opacidad y lock mode: decirlo.

<b>H2: Annotate</b>

- Flechas, líneas, cajas, elipses, resaltados, mano alzada, etiquetas numeradas, lupa, texto, bocadillos, sellos de imagen; guarda una copia PNG. Para tapar datos sensibles, Redact Image (⇧⌘R) con caras/texto detectados y blur. ⚠️ Flujo directo desde la ventana Capture a Redact: no documentado.

<b>H2: Record your screen with auto zoom</b>

- Record Screen/Area/Window; 60 o 30 fps; zoom automático a los clics (empieza 0,4 s antes, vuelve tras 1,2 s sin actividad, 2× por defecto); cámara, micrófono y audio del sistema; transcripción en el Mac; export MP4 (H.264/HEVC, hasta 4K60) o GIF. Enlazar /screen-studio-alternative/.

## 7. Bloque citable (AEO) + FAQ + schema

- <b>Is there a free alternative to CleanShot X?</b> Yes. Ferrocast is a free, open-source (MIT) Mac app that takes area, window, screen and scrolling screenshots, copies the text in a capture on-device, pins captures above other windows, annotates them and records the screen with automatic zoom on clicks. It has no cloud upload or capture history, and screen capture needs macOS 14 or later.
- <b>How much does CleanShot X cost?</b> According to [cleanshot.com](http://cleanshot.com) (28 October 2026), CleanShot Basic is a \$35 one-time purchase with one year of updates, renewable at \$19 a year, and CleanShot Pro is \$10 per user per month billed annually. There is no free trial, but there is a 30-day money-back guarantee.
- <b>Can I copy text from a screenshot on a Mac without uploading it?</b> Yes. In Ferrocast's Capture window, Copy Text (⌘T) reads the text with Apple's Vision framework on your Mac and puts it on the clipboard. The image is not sent anywhere.

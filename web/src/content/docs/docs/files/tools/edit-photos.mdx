The photo editor shows nine sliders (brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, clarity, sharpen, grain, denoise, dehaze) with the preview recomputed on a reduced copy.

Press <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> on an image result; <kbd>↵</kbd> writes the full-size copy and never overwrites. Typed: `edit brightness 20 contrast 10 saturation -30 warmth 15 clarity 40 sharpen 30 grain 20 denoise 30 dehaze 50`. One-step presets: black & white, brighter, darker, more contrast.

:::caution[Limits]
Rendering was checked by screenshot; the sliders are not tested in a real session. The preview uses a reduced copy.
:::

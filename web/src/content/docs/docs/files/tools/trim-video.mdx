The trim viewer shows the frame at the cursor with a timeline, start and end markers, and steps by frame or second. Pressing <kbd>↵</kbd> writes an exact, frame-accurate cut (re-encoded).

Press <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>V</kbd> on a video result. <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> step a frame, <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> a second, <kbd>I</kbd> and <kbd>O</kbd> set markers, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump, and you can click the bar. Typed: `trim 0:10-0:30` (stream copy, so cuts land on keyframes).

:::caution[Limits]
Needs ffmpeg. Keys and bar dragging are not tested in a real session; the latency of the preview was not measured.
:::

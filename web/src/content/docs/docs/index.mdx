Ferrocast is a launcher for macOS written in Rust. It searches apps, commands and files, does math, currency and time-zone conversion, keeps clipboard history, expands snippets, manages windows, asks AI models, and converts and edits files locally from a drag-and-drop wheel.

Press <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> from any app to open it. It is free, MIT licensed, needs no account, and runs on Apple Silicon Macs only. It installs with Homebrew, which compiles it on your Mac.

## Who it is for

People who want a fast, keyboard-driven launcher whose data stays in JSON files on their own disk, and who are fine with Script Commands instead of an extension store. An extension store and sync between Macs are not included.

## What it is not

Missing today: an extension store and sync between Macs. React extensions, Search Menu Bar Items and AI context (selection or screen) are not included.
